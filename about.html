About

HearHere started with a question: why do podcast apps feel cluttered, slow, or missing key features?

Every feature exists because it matters. The smart caching that makes the app fast. The download manager that works reliably. The sleep timer that fades gently. The chapter support for structured shows.

HearHere is built on the PodcastIndex.org API, supporting independent podcasting.

Currently in beta via TestFlight. App Store launch coming soon.
