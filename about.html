Built by podcast enthusiasts, for podcast enthusiasts

HearHere started with a simple question: why do so many podcast apps feel cluttered, slow, or missing key features? We set out to build the app we wanted to use—fast, beautiful, and packed with the features that actually matter.

Every feature in HearHere exists because we use it ourselves. The smart caching system that makes the app blazingly fast. The download manager that handles offline listening perfectly. The sleep timer that fades out gently. The chapter support for structured shows. We obsessed over these details because they transform good listening into great listening.

HearHere is built on open standards using the PodcastIndex.org API, supporting the independent podcast ecosystem. We believe in podcasting's open nature and we're committed to keeping HearHere free, fast, and focused on what matters: your listening experience.

Current Status

HearHere is currently in beta testing via TestFlight. We're gathering feedback from early users, fixing bugs, and adding polish before our official App Store launch. Want to be part of the journey? Join our beta program.

Our Mission

Make listening to podcasts effortless and enjoyable. No bloat, no distractions—just the features you need wrapped in a design you'll love.
