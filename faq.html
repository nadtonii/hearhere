Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about HearHere

Is HearHere available now?

HearHere is currently in beta testing through Apple's TestFlight program. You can request access to the beta on our website. We're planning an App Store launch after gathering feedback and polishing the experience.

Is HearHere really free?

Yes! HearHere is completely free with all features unlocked, both in beta and when we launch. No premium tiers, no subscriptions, no ads—ever.

How do I join the beta?

Visit our Beta page and request access with your email. We'll send you a TestFlight invitation within 24-48 hours. You'll need to install the TestFlight app from the App Store first.

Which platforms does HearHere support?

HearHere is currently available for iOS (iPhone and iPad) via TestFlight. We're focused on perfecting the iOS experience before considering other platforms.

Will my beta data transfer when HearHere launches?

Yes! Your subscriptions, favorites, downloads, and settings will all carry over when HearHere moves to the App Store. We recommend using the export feature occasionally as a backup.

Can I import my subscriptions from another app?

Yes! HearHere supports data import. You can export from most major podcast apps and import your subscriptions easily.

Does HearHere support offline listening?

Absolutely. Download episodes for offline playback with our smart download manager. Downloads happen in the background and you can manage storage easily.

What is Podcasting 2.0?

Podcasting 2.0 introduces enhanced features like chapters, which HearHere fully supports. Navigate through structured episodes with ease.

How much storage do downloads use?

HearHere shows you exactly how much storage your downloads use. You can delete individual episodes or clear all downloads with one tap.

Can I customize playback speed?

Yes! Choose speeds from 0.5x to 2.0x, and set your preferred default speed in settings.

Does HearHere work with CarPlay / Lock Screen?

Yes! HearHere integrates with iOS media controls, so you can control playback from the lock screen, control center, and CarPlay.

How do I use the sleep timer?

Open the full player and tap the sleep timer icon. Choose a duration (5m, 10m, 15m, 30m, 1h) or select "End of Episode" to stop after the current episode finishes.

What is Voice Boost?

Voice Boost is an audio enhancement feature that makes voices clearer and more prominent—perfect for podcasts with varying audio quality.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

We love bug reports! You can report issues through TestFlight's built-in feedback system or email us at beta@hearhere.app. Please include as much detail as possible.

Can I suggest features?

Absolutely! Beta testers have a direct line to development. Email us your suggestions at feedback@hearhere.app. Many beta features have come from user requests.

Still have questions?

Join our beta and reach out directly. We're here to help!
