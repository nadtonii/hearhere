FAQ

Is HearHere available now?

HearHere is in beta testing through TestFlight. Request access on the Beta page.

Is it free?

Yes. No subscriptions, no ads, no premium tiers. Ever.

How do I join the beta?

Request access with your email. You'll receive a TestFlight invitation within 24-48 hours.

Which platforms?

iOS (iPhone and iPad). TestFlight required for beta.

Will my beta data transfer to the App Store version?

Yes. Your subscriptions, favorites, and settings carry over. Use the export feature for occasional backups.

Can I import subscriptions from another app?

Yes. Most podcast apps support export, and HearHere can import that data.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Download episodes and HearHere automatically uses local files when available.

What is Podcasting 2.0?

Enhanced podcast features like chapters. HearHere fully supports chapter navigation.

How much storage do downloads use?

HearHere shows exact storage usage. Delete episodes individually or clear all downloads at once.

Can I customize playback speed?

Yes. Choose from 0.5x to 2.0x and set your default preference.

Does it work with CarPlay?

Yes. Full integration with iOS media controls, lock screen, and CarPlay.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap the timer icon in the full player. Choose a duration (5m, 10m, 15m, 30m, 1h) or "End of Episode."

What is Voice Boost?

An audio enhancement feature that makes voices clearer—useful for shows with varying audio quality.

How do I report bugs?

Use TestFlight's feedback system or email beta@hearhere.app with details.

Can I suggest features?

Absolutely. Email feedback@hearhere.app.
