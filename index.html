Podcasts have been there for years. Commutes. Dinner. Flights. Falling asleep. They filled the quiet moments of modern life.

HearHere is a podcast player focused on getting the basics right. Built by one person who listens to podcasts every day.

Private by design

Every podcast app wants to be a platform. They want to know what you listen to, when you listen, how long you stay. They want to serve you ads, push recommendations, keep you "engaged."

HearHere is different. It's a library app, not a feed app. You choose what goes in it. You decide how it's organized. Nobody's watching, nobody's optimizing, nobody's monetizing your attention.

You are not the product. For real.

Everything stays on your device. Your subscriptions. Your listening history. Your searches.

Optional iCloud sync keeps your library in sync across devices—encrypted and private to you.

HearHere doesn't collect your data. There's no advertising business. No tracking. No selling insights. What you listen to is nobody's business but yours.

Built for listening.

HearHere feels fast because it is fast. Navigation makes sense. Controls are where you expect them. Nothing gets in the way of what you're here to do.

Your library looks as good as it works because that's what a podcast app should feel like.
