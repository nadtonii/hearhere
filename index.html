Smart Discovery

Search thousands of podcasts instantly. Browse trending shows or explore by category. Your recent searches are always ready.

Intelligent Playback

Variable speed (0.5x–2.0x), customizable skip intervals, voice boost, and automatic position saving. Pick up exactly where you left off.

Offline Listening

Download episodes in the background. HearHere manages storage automatically and switches to local files when available.

Chapter Support

Navigate structured shows with full Podcasting 2.0 chapter support. Jump between sections or browse the chapter list.

Sleep Timer

Set a custom duration or end-of-episode mode. The audio fades out gently so you don't wake up to silence.

Organization

Unlimited subscriptions, episode favorites, playback history, and a home screen that shows what matters: continue listening and latest episodes.

A Simple Approach

HearHere is built by someone who listens to podcasts every day and wanted an app that just works.

No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions.

Smart caching makes everything fast. Downloads work reliably. The interface stays out of your way.

HearHere uses the PodcastIndex.org API, supporting the open podcast ecosystem.
