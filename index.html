Podcasts have been there for years—during commutes, making dinner, on flights, falling asleep. They filled the quiet moments and made mundane tasks bearable.

HearHere is a podcast player focused on doing the basics right. Built by one person who listens to podcasts every day.

What It Does

The home screen shows what you care about—pick up where you left off, your subscribed shows are right there, and the latest episodes are already loaded. Search is fast and the mini player stays accessible without getting in the way.

Thoughtful design comes before everything. Careful attention to how episodes load, how the player transitions, and where buttons are placed. The goal was to make an app that gets out of the way and lets you focus on listening.

No social features. No gamification. No algorithmic feeds. Just a thoughtfully designed tool for listening to podcasts.

Why It's Free

HearHere is free because that's how it should be. No paywalls, no premium tiers, no ads. Just the app, working the way it should.
