HearHere

Privacy & Policy

HearHere doesn't collect your data. Everything stays on your device.

What's Stored Locally

Your podcast subscriptions, episode favorites, playback positions, downloaded files, and preferences all live in your device's storage. Delete the app and everything goes with it.

No account required. No cloud sync. No server storing your listening history.

What Gets Sent

When you search for podcasts or load episode information, HearHere connects to PodcastIndex.org to fetch that data. Your search queries reach their servers, but HearHere doesn't log them or store them.

That's it. No analytics. No tracking. No third-party SDKs.

Your Control

HearHere can't see your data. There's no access to it. Privacy by design.
