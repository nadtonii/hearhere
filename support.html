Getting Help

Every email gets a response. Include what's broken, what you were doing when it happened, and which podcast or episode if relevant.

TestFlight has a built-in feedback button that attaches system logs—use that if you can, but direct email works too.

Common Issues

Downloads keep failing

Usually a network issue, but sometimes certain podcast feeds have problems. Email the specific show and episode.

App crashes

Send crash details through TestFlight or describe the steps that trigger it.

Playback won't start

Try force-quitting and reopening. If that doesn't work, email details—which episode, whether it's downloaded or streaming, what happens when you press play.

Can't find a podcast

HearHere uses PodcastIndex.org's directory. If a show isn't indexed there, it won't appear. Most podcasts get picked up within 24 hours. If something's genuinely missing, let us know.

Import/export not working

Email your export file if you're comfortable sharing it, or describe what's happening.

Beta Reality

This is beta software. Bugs happen. When they do, fixes come quickly.
